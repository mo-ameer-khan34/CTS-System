Privacy & POPIA summary

This summary explains how TRACE handles personal information submitted with a captain or constable registration request.

Details such as your name, South African ID number, date of birth, contact information, officer ID, rank and department are used to review your registration and administer your account and access to TRACE.

Personal information must be handled in accordance with the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA), including appropriate safeguards and applicable retention requirements. You may request access to or correction of your information, or raise a privacy concern with the system administrator or the Information Regulator.

This is a short summary, not the full privacy policy. Contact the system administrator for further information about processing and retention.
