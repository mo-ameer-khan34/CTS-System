Complainant Portal
HOW IT WORKS
01

Report & Record

Constables capture docket information, upload documents, and run AI validation on affidavits before submission.

02

Investigate & Progress

Cases move through defined statuses. Every view, edit, and status change is logged to the immutable audit trail.

03

Oversee & Close

Captains review investigations, flag irregularities, and formally close cases after proceedings are complete.

04

Appeal & Resolve

Complainants can submit written appeals on closed cases. Admins review and decide, ensuring full transparency.
